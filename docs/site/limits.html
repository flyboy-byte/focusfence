Limits

  • DNS-level blocking only. It sees host names, not page paths over HTTPS. Section rules use the accessibility service for that.
  • The phone's owner can get around it. Someone with ADB or who changes Android settings can turn things off. The point is that partners are told, not that it is impossible.
  • Android can drop Always-on VPN or kill background work. The app reminds you and reports gaps instead of hiding them. Android's "Block connections without VPN" is incompatible.
  • Screen checks can be wrong. They sample the screen, so they can miss things, and they can raise false alarms. Their accuracy has not been measured.
  • Time budgets are estimates inferred from DNS activity.
  • New group members see only recent history: report slices are kept for 9 days, alerts for 14.
  • Barely tried on a phone: strict mode, the weekly report email and several alert kinds. Test what you rely on.
  • A browser that joined by code gets no alerts, only the report. For alerts, join in the app or from an emailed invite.
  • Apps you let skip the filter are not filtered at all.

Privacy

Written from what the code does on 2026-11-23 (app 2.9.0). It is checked again whenever a release adds something the app observes. No ads, no analytics, no third-party tracking code.

With every optional layer off

The filter runs on the phone. It answers domain-name lookups itself and passes allowed ones to your network's own DNS server, or to Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) / Google (8.8.8.8) over DoH if that fails, or to the server you choose in Settings. Nothing about you is sent to us. A local activity log is kept on the phone (about 32 MB at most, oldest files removed first). It leaves the phone only when you export it yourself.

If you add a partner or group (an accountability server)

This is off until you enter a server address. Then:

  • The phone uploads its activity records every 15 minutes, signed with a key that never leaves the phone. The server can read these records (domains, apps, alerts). This was a deliberate choice for now.
  • Screenshots, if you share them, are encrypted to your partner's key; the server cannot read them.
  • App-to-app pairing and groups use "blind mailboxes": the server stores only encrypted messages under an identifier it cannot link to a person, and keeps a hash of the access token.
  • The server also holds: the report token for each partner, partner email addresses (and phone numbers if SMS is used), and the phone's public key.
  • Who runs the server can see what it holds. You can run your own (how) instead of trusting someone else's.
  • Mailboxes keep the last 300 messages for at most 30 days. Uploaded records are rotated by size.
  • An email address you give for a partner receives a confirmation first and is used only for that partner's alerts and weekly reports. Alerts are rate-limited.
  • Email is sent through Resend. SMS, only if the operator switches it on, goes through Twilio or Telnyx.

What a partner or group member sees

A partner sees your reports and alerts. In a group, every member sees every filtered member by default; members who are only partners are not watched. Joining a group does not grant trust: each person approves a new member after comparing a 12-digit fingerprint in person or on a call. Anyone can leave, and loosening actions go through the same friction (delay or partner PIN) you set.

Deleting things

  • Settings, Logs and diagnostics, Clear: deletes the local logs (this is itself recorded and partners are told).
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything on the phone. The app is never prevented from being uninstalled.
  • Removing a partner or leaving a group stops future sharing. Data already on a server is removed by its operator; ask them.
